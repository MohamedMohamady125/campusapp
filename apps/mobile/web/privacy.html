CampusConnect Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

CampusConnect ("we", "us") is a verified-student campus platform for buying and selling with peers, peer tutoring, community chats, and coordinating on-campus food runs. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We collect the minimum needed to run the service and we never sell your personal data.

Who can use CampusConnect

Accounts are limited to verified students using a campus email address. CampusConnect is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your campus email address, display name, and (optionally) year, major, and bio. Used to create and secure your account and to show you as a verified peer.
  • Content you create — listings, messages, chat posts, ratings, reports, and any photos you upload. Used to provide the core features and to keep the community safe.
  • Approximate and precise location — only while you are actively part of a food run that is en route, to show live runner progress to the people on that run. Location is used in the foreground for that purpose and is not tracked in the background or sold.
  • Device and log data — basic technical information (app version, error/diagnostic logs) used to keep the app reliable and secure.

How we use information

  • Provide and operate the marketplace, tutoring, chats, food runs, and messaging features.
  • Verify student identity and maintain one portable reputation across features.
  • Protect users through moderation, reporting, and abuse prevention.
  • Diagnose problems and improve reliability and performance.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Content you post (listings, chat messages, reputation) is visible to other verified students as intended by the feature you use. We share data with service providers who host and operate the app on our behalf (for example, cloud hosting and image storage), under contracts that limit their use of it. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect the safety of users.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can request account deletion at any time by emailing us at the address below; we will soft-delete and then scrub your personal content, subject to any legal retention obligations.

Security

Passwords are hashed with argon2id, data is transmitted over TLS, and access is protected by authentication and authorization checks. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices

  • You can turn off location permission in your device settings; live food-run progress simply won't display.
  • You can edit or remove content you posted.
  • You can request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions or data requests: support@campusconnect.app
