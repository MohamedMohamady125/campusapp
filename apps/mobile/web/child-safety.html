Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 22 September 2026

CampusConnect is a verified-student campus platform for coordinating food runs and connecting with peers. We have a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page sets out our published standards for preventing, detecting, and responding to such content and conduct, in line with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

Our commitment

  • We prohibit CSAE and CSAM of any kind on CampusConnect. There are no exceptions.
  • We prohibit any content or behavior that sexualizes, grooms, endangers, or exploits minors.
  • We act quickly to remove violating content, terminate offending accounts, and report to the appropriate authorities.

Who can use CampusConnect

Accounts are limited to verified students using a valid campus email address, and the app is intended for users aged 18 and over. CampusConnect is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly permit minors to create accounts.

How we prevent abuse

  • Verified identity. Every account is tied to a verified campus email, so users are accountable real peers, not anonymous strangers.
  • Reporting tools. Users can report any user, message, or piece of content directly in the app. Reports of child-safety concerns are prioritized for review.
  • Moderation. Reported content is reviewed and, where it violates this policy, removed. Offending accounts are suspended or banned.
  • Blocking removal at the source. Confirmed CSAM is removed and preserved as required by law for reporting to authorities.

How to report child-safety concerns

  • In the app: use the report action available on a user, message, or run to flag content or behavior for review.
  • By email: contact us at mohamadmohamady125@gmail.com. A designated point of contact is available to respond to child-safety reports and to speak to our CSAM prevention practices.

How we respond and report to authorities

When we become aware of apparent CSAM or CSAE, we remove the content, disable the responsible account, and report to the relevant regional and national authorities, including the U.S. National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) at report.cybertip.org where applicable, and to other competent authorities as required by law. We cooperate with law enforcement requests consistent with applicable legal process.

Compliance

CampusConnect complies with all applicable child-safety laws and reporting obligations in the jurisdictions where it operates. We review these standards regularly and update them as our product and the law evolve.
