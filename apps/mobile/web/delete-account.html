Delete your CampusConnect account

Last updated: 14 September 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your CampusConnect account and the data associated with it. CampusConnect is a verified-student campus platform for buying and selling, peer tutoring, community chats, and food runs.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@campusconnect.app from the campus email address on your CampusConnect account.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my account".
  3. We confirm ownership of the account, then process the deletion.

Your account is disabled promptly on request, and your personal data is deleted within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Your account profile — email address, display name, year, major, bio, and avatar.
  • Your personal content — listings, messages, chat posts, ratings you wrote, and uploaded photos.
  • Any location data associated with your food-run activity.

What may be kept

  • Records we are legally required to retain, kept only for the minimum period required by law and then deleted.
  • Aggregated or anonymized data that no longer identifies you.
  • Content that is essential context for another user's safety report or an active moderation case may be retained until that case is resolved.

Contact

Questions about deletion or your data: support@campusconnect.app. See also our Privacy Policy.
